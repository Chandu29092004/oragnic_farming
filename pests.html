🐛 Pest Management

Identify common crop pests and learn organic management methods.

📅 12 August 2026

🌱 Organic Pest Management

Integrated pest management combines monitoring, prevention, biological control and other suitable practices to reduce crop damage. In organic farming, farmers can use appropriate biological and cultural methods while protecting beneficial organisms.

🐜

Aphids

Vegetables

Small sap-sucking insects that can cause curling, yellowing and reduced plant growth.

Signs: Clusters of small insects on young shoots

Organic approach: Encourage natural predators such as ladybirds

Prevention: Regular crop inspection and weed management

🐛

Tomato Fruit Borer

Tomato

Caterpillars can feed on tomato fruits and damage developing crops.

Signs: Holes and feeding damage on fruits

Organic approach: Monitor regularly and use suitable biological control methods

Prevention: Remove severely affected plant material

🐛

Rice Stem Borer

Rice

Larvae damage rice stems and may cause dead-heart or white-ear symptoms.

Signs: Dead tillers or white panicles

Organic approach: Use monitoring and appropriate biological control techniques

Prevention: Field sanitation and balanced crop management

🦋

Whitefly

Tomato

Small insects that feed on plant sap and may contribute to the spread of plant diseases.

Signs: Small white insects flying from leaves

Organic approach: Use monitoring and encourage beneficial insects

Prevention: Remove heavily affected leaves where appropriate

🐛

Brinjal Shoot Borer

Brinjal

Larvae bore into young shoots and fruits, reducing plant growth and crop quality.

Signs: Wilting shoots and holes in fruits

Organic approach: Regularly remove affected shoots and fruits

Prevention: Frequent crop monitoring

🐞

Mealybug

Vegetables

Sap-feeding insects covered with a waxy coating that can weaken plants.

Signs: White cotton-like clusters on plant parts

Organic approach: Remove affected parts and encourage natural enemies

Prevention: Inspect new plants before introducing them

🌿 Organic Pest Control Methods

Use an integrated approach rather than relying on a single control method. Identify the pest first, monitor crop damage and choose an appropriate management strategy.

🐞 Biological Control

Conserve beneficial insects and other natural enemies that help regulate pest populations.

🌱 Cultural Control

Use crop rotation, field sanitation, healthy planting material and suitable cultivation practices.

🔍 Regular Monitoring

Inspect leaves, stems and fruits regularly so pest problems can be identified early.

💡 Pest Prevention Tips

🔍 Inspect crops regularly: Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruits for early signs of pest activity.
🌾 Practice crop rotation: Rotating suitable crops can help reduce the buildup of some crop-specific pests.
🐞 Protect beneficial insects: Avoid unnecessary actions that could harm natural pest enemies.
🌿 Maintain field hygiene: Remove suitable sources of pest infestation and manage weeds appropriately.
