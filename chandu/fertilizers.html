🌿 Organic Fertilizers

Learn about natural fertilizers for healthy organic crops.

📅 12 August 2026

🌱 Why Use Organic Fertilizers?

Organic fertilizers are made from natural materials such as plant residues, animal manure and compost. They improve soil fertility, support beneficial soil organisms and provide nutrients to crops.

♻️

Compost

Compost

Decomposed organic materials that improve soil structure and provide nutrients.

Best for: Vegetables and fruits

Application: Mix with soil before planting

Benefit: Improves soil fertility

🪱

Vermicompost

Compost

Nutrient-rich organic fertilizer produced using earthworms and decomposed organic matter.

Best for: Vegetables and flowering plants

Application: Apply around plant roots

Benefit: Improves nutrient availability

🐄

Cow Manure

Animal Manure

Well-decomposed cow manure is a traditional source of organic nutrients for farm soil.

Best for: Field crops and vegetables

Application: Mix well-decomposed manure into soil

Benefit: Adds organic matter

🌿

Neem Cake

Plant Based

Neem seed cake is an organic soil amendment commonly used in crop production.

Best for: Vegetables and field crops

Application: Mix into soil near the root zone

Benefit: Adds nutrients and organic matter

🌱

Green Manure

Plant Based

Green manure crops are grown and incorporated into soil to add organic matter.

Best for: Rice and field crops

Application: Incorporate suitable green plants into soil

Benefit: Improves soil organic matter

💧

Jeevamrut

Liquid Fertilizer

A traditional fermented farm input used in some natural farming practices.

Best for: Various crops

Application: Use according to an appropriate farm practice

Benefit: Supports soil biological activity

🌾 Crop-wise Recommendations

Select suitable organic inputs according to your crop, soil condition and local farming practices. Always consider soil testing and recommended application rates.

🍅 Tomato

Compost and well-decomposed manure can be incorporated into the soil before planting.

🌾 Rice

Green manure and compost can contribute organic matter to rice-growing soils.

🥬 Spinach

Mature compost can be used as part of soil fertility management.

💡 Organic Fertilizer Tips

🌱 Use mature compost: Make sure compost is properly decomposed before applying it to crops.
🧪 Test your soil: Soil testing helps determine nutrient requirements more accurately.
💧 Avoid over-application: More fertilizer does not always mean better crop growth.
♻️ Recycle farm waste: Crop residues can be managed through appropriate composting practices.
